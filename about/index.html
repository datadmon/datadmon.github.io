About Daemon Data

Daemon Data is the publisher behind the Apify Store account data_daemon. It builds and maintains pay-per-record data APIs for sources that buyers ask for and that have no official API: Indian recruiter job boards, employer career sites on Oracle Recruiting Cloud, Indian IPO and stock-market figures, the Dubai Land Department transaction register, UK insolvency notices from The Gazette and Luma event calendars.

How the APIs work

Each API is an Apify Actor: a hosted program that reads the source website's own public JSON endpoints or pages, normalises the records (ISO dates, numeric values, stable ids) and writes them to a dataset you can download as JSON, CSV or Excel or read over the Apify API. Runs take seconds to a few minutes. There is no login to the source site, no proxy to manage and no browser automation; the Actors use plain HTTP, which is why they are cheap and rarely break.

What is and is not collected

The Actors return the data the source publishes to every visitor: job postings, company profiles, IPO figures, registered property transactions, insolvency notices, public events. They deliberately leave out personal contact details that some sources expose (recruiter names and phone numbers, event guest lists). Dubai Land Department data is published for reuse by the department; Gazette notices are Crown copyright under the Open Government Licence; the other sources are read as public web pages and should be used in line with their terms of use and with data-protection law. Grey-market premium figures are unofficial indicators and not investment advice.

Prices

Every API is priced per record returned, from $0.80 per 1,000 records. Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month, which covers a trial and several small daily feeds. Prices on this site are FREE-plan list prices read from the live listings on the date shown in the footer; Scale and Business plans pay 15% to 30% less. Scheduled price changes are shown on the API's page.

Support and contact

Bug reports and field requests: the Issues tab of the Actor on Apify (fastest) or GitHub issues. Issues are usually answered within a day. Custom variants of an Actor, or a new source in the same style, are available on request through the same channels.
